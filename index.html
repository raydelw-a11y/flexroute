⚡ How to Enable 1-Tap iPhone Shortcut (iOS Shortcuts)

0 sec

With the native iPhone Shortcuts app you can double-tap the back of your phone inside Amazon Flex to capture the screen and open FlexRoute with all stops loaded:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone and tap + (New Shortcut).
  2. Add the "Take Screenshot" action.
  3. Add the "Extract Text from Image" action.
  4. Add the "URL Encode" action passing the Text from Image.
  5. Add the "Open URLs" action with this address:
    https://raydelw-a11y.github.io/flexroute/?import=[URL Encoded Text]
Want to append multiple screens in a row? Use ?append=1&import=... to accumulate stops.
